## Context

See proposal.md — Why. Stage 1 of a three-stage migration toward one composition mechanism. Today: four isolation families with separate plan classes and renderer arms, dispatched by `classify_isolation` (`engine/isolation.py:92`); the regroup primitive (DEV-1825/29) already implements producer + attach + substitution but stops at depth 1 — producers may not need CTEs (`stage_planner.py:1452`), row+combined may not coexist (`stage_planner.py:1354`, `generator.py:1601`), and discovery only finds bare partitioned `AggregateKey`s. Hard constraints: all currently-passing goldens byte-identical (divergences individually approved); fail-closed for unrouted shapes; every stage-1 interface source-agnostic so stages 2–3 are routing migrations.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** lift the full local `partition_by` guard list on the generalized primitive; establish the measure⇔dimension symmetry axiom; recursive rendering with CTE-hoist; explicit filter placement; structural cardinality validation at the attach.

**Non-Goals:** row-attach × transform-chain coexistence (stage 1a, DEV-1837); migrating `_wm_`/`_rk_`/`_cm_` triggers (stages 2–3); cross-phase attach-dedup/interning (stage 2); cross-model partitioned aggregates and target-rooted producers (stage 3); retiring `classify_isolation` (stage 3); transform-family-internal gaps (`time_shift` over composite inputs etc. — separate issues); folding the root SELECT's flat residue into node discipline (committed as stage 4, DEV-1838).

## Decisions

- **D1 — `PlannedQuery` is the recursive node; no new IR.** Recursion already exists ad hoc (`rerooted_plan`, `producer_plan`, sibling stages); a parallel relational IR would mean a dual representation for the whole migration and re-derivation of ~9k lines of fought-for renderer behavior. *Rejected:* dedicated algebraic IR — becomes easier, not harder, after stages 1–3 if ever still wanted.
- **D2 — Recursive render contract with CTE-hoist via allocator threading (Codex F4).** Producer rendering returns CTE entries + select; nested renders receive the *parent's* `AliasAllocator` instead of a fresh one (`generator.py:1459`), so all names — including a producer's internal base and step CTEs — are globally unique by construction; `depends_on` edges use allocated names. Byte-identity holds because allocator behavior is unchanged when nothing nests. *Rejected:* structured CTE graph with opaque ids and late naming — same guarantee, a fourth representation.
- **D3 — Discovery = recursive grain classification, not "maximal subexpression" (Codex F1).** Outcomes per subexpression: `self_contained(grain)` atomic producer root (a partitioned aggregate, or a transform forced to its input's grain by dimension context), `consumer_composable` (arithmetic/scalar/CASE over attached columns — one attach per root, composition stays in the consumer), `requires_nested_attach` (aggregate over an attached value — fail closed), `grain_conflict` never arises because roots are atomic.
- **D4 — Context-grain rule for transforms (Codex F6).** A transform evaluates at the grain of its containing context: as a measure, at query grain over the attached (broadcast) value — mirroring how windowed-in-composite will work; inside a dimension expression, at the producer grain of its grained input (circularity forces this). `rank(x:sum(partition_by=region))` therefore ranks result rows as a measure and ranks regions as a dimension — both new shapes, no status quo broken. Combined attaches keep the DEV-1739 rule that partition keys must be query dimensions, so join-backs always find their host slots.
- **D5 — Windowed producers get an explicit synthesized time dimension (Codex F2).** `partition_by=` + `window=` synthesizes a `TimeTruncKey` at the consumer's active time-dimension granularity into the producer (declared main time key, included verbatim in attach keys) — the windowed machinery requires a real interned time slot (`stage_planner.py:536`) and regroup producers currently set `main_time_key=None` (`stage_planner.py:1289`). Ambiguous/multi-axis shapes fail closed.
- **D6 — Ranked producers carry an explicit ranking key (Codex F5).** Resolved against the original expression during producer synthesis, never re-derived from the synthetic producer's row slots (`ranked_planner.py:143` scans row keys and a temporal partition key could hijack ordering).
- **D7 — Filter placement is decided pre-substitution and carried explicitly (Codex F3, F7).** Substitution lowers expression phase to ROW by design (`regroup_planner.py:245`), so placement cannot be re-derived from the substituted tree. Per top-level conjunct: resolve every operand to its availability scope (base-row / base-grouped / combined / post), route to the earliest common scope via an explicit placement field the generator consumes verbatim (generalizing `outer_where_filter_ids`); no common scope → clean error. Legality is scope-resolved, never syntactic ("plain aggregate" is not automatically combined-available — windowed/ranked values live in their own CTEs).
- **D8 — Attach plans declare the producer's unique key; the planner validates it (Codex F8).** Join pairs must cover exactly the declared key; an empty pair list requires a structurally provable one-row producer (no dimensions). This makes the cardinality invariant a checked property instead of a test-only assertion.
- **D9 — Order-form discrimination (issue comment).** An order expression resolving to a computed-dimension alias keeps row-attach routing; one that directly is/contains the raw partitioned aggregate routes to a combined attach (legal under coexistence). The leaked-placeholder `ValueError` becomes unreachable.
- **D10 — Same-aggregate-both-phases ships as duplicate producers.** Cross-phase dedup is stage 2's general attach-dedup pass; correctness first.
- **D11 — Coexistence with unmigrated families (`generator.py:1593`) lifts now.** The combined SELECT's join-back machinery (`joinback_specs`) is already shared; per-pair executed-value tests are the net. Migration of those families remains stages 2–3.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Allocator threading perturbs existing names] → allocator behavior is identical when no producer nests; full-suite golden byte-identity is the tripwire.
- [Guard lifted but a sub-shape silently wrong] → per-scenario executed-value tests on SQLite + DuckDB, not goldens alone; guard tests in both directions (lifted shapes work, remaining errors raise exact messages).
- [Placement field and phase machinery drift] → placement decided in one pass, generator consumes it verbatim; scope-closure validation (`SLAYER_VALIDATE_SCOPES`) on all new emissions.
- [Recursive discovery meets a shape nobody anticipated] → classification is exhaustive with `requires_nested_attach`/no-common-scope failing closed with clean messages.
- [Producer duplication cost (D10)] → narrow shape (same aggregate, both roles); resolved by stage 2 dedup.

## Migration Plan

Closure axiom governing the arc: **any grain-legal dimension composes with any legal measure** — every fail-closed coexistence guard is a temporary migration artifact with an issue attached, never a permanent boundary.

Stage 1 (this change) → **Stage 1a (DEV-1837) — computed dimension × transform-measure coexistence:** transform measures (`time_shift` and kin) are steps over the query-grain result, not attaches, so no family migration dissolves this pair; lift the row-attach guard for the `transform_layers`-only case in both render paths, and introduce the dimension-family × measure-family compatibility-matrix test (supported pairs execute, guarded pairs xfail pointing at their issue). → **Stage 2 (DEV-1835) — local family unification + attach-dedup:** migrate `_wm_` (range-join producer) and local `_rk_` (row_number producer) onto the primitive, delete their renderer arms and `classify_isolation` branches; DEV-1504 G4/G5/G7-family and `time_shift`-over-ranked dissolve; general attach-dedup subsumes cross-phase interning. → **Stage 3 (DEV-1836) — cross-model unification:** target-rooted producers via `reroot_value_key`; migrate `_cm_` (incl. rerooted/filtered-local), cross-model `_rk_`, cross-model partitioned aggregates (delete `_narrow_shared_grain_to_partition` and the local-only routing in `regroup_planner.py:140`); retire `classify_isolation` dispatch; cross-model sources become legal in dimension expressions and `window=`; intermediate-hop guards fall. → **Stage 4 (DEV-1838) — node discipline:** fold the root SELECT's flat residue and the transform-step chain into the recursive node (base → aggregate → combined → steps → post, each consuming only the previous node's schema); one transform-chain implementation; the `as_cte_body` deferrals lift via the D2 CTE-hoist; exit criterion — the coexistence guard list is empty and the matrix has no xfails. Rollback within stage 1: guards are removed per-shape in separate commits, each revertable without touching the mechanics.
